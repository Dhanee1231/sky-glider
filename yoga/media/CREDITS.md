# Yoga Coach media credits

All media in this folder is self-hosted, loaded only when used, and cached on the device for offline use.
Videos were trimmed to short loops, cropped to remove on-screen text/counters, muted and re-encoded (H.264, 25 fps).
Photos were resized and are shown with a slow "Ken Burns" zoom.
